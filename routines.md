This is a page about Routines.

Lindy Hop is most commonly done as a social dance. It focuses on embracing the spirit of improvisation that defines the jazz music that is a core element of the dance. However another way to dance the Lindy Hop is in routines. These are typically prechoreographed completely with multiple dancers coordinating to do the same or complementary moves. They often include [aerials](/aerials/) and formations to provide an additional level of flair. Here's a collection of some of the most popular routines. Many date back to the early days of the dance and were choreographed and first performed by the [first generation of Lindy Hop dancers](/historical_figures/).
