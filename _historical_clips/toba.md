This association formed the circuit on which black performers were able to travel and perform in the 1920 United States. 

There is a [T.O.B.A. break step](/jazz/toba_break) which is very commonly danced. 

And many of the most visible swing band leaders and musicians worked on this tour, including: Fats Waller, Louis Armstrong, Duke Ellington, Chick Webb, Cab Calloway, and Count Basie.

More information can be found on [Wikipedia at Theatre Owners Booking Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theatre_Owners_Booking_Association). 

There's also a [history of T.O.B.A from Musical Geography](https://musicalgeography.org/history-of-t-o-b-a/) as well as an [overview of some of the black women who were on the circuit](https://musicalgeography.org/project/mapping-black-women-in-vaudeville-t-o-b-a-circuit/).
