Norma Miller was one of the early Lindy Hop dancers at the [Savoy Ballroom.](/historical_clips/savoy_ballroom/)
She earned the nickname 'Queen of Swing'. 

Find out more about her on [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norma_Miller) and she is featured by the [National Endowment for the Arts](https://www.arts.gov/honors/heritage/norma-miller) as well as [here](https://www.frankiemanningfoundation.org/norma-miller) by the Frankie Manning Foundation.

She member of  [Whitey's Lindy Hoppers](/historical_clips/whiteys_lindy_hoppers)

She appeared in several films over the years. [Here's her IMDB profile](https://www.imdb.com/name/nm0589073/). 

She published several books including:

* [Swingin' at the Savoy](https://books.google.com/books/about/Swingin_at_the_Savoy.html)
* [Swing, Baby, Swing](https://www.google.com/books/edition/Swing_Baby_Swing/k5zfzwEACAAJ)
* [The Redd Foxx Encyclopedia of Black Humor](https://www.google.com/books/edition/The_Redd_Foxx_Encyclopedia_of_Black_Humo/YTfYAAAAMAAJ)

She passed away in 2019 at age 99.
You can find her obituary in many promenant locations such as the [New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/2019/05/06/obituaries/norma-miller-dead.html), [Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/local/obituaries/norma-miller-lindy-hop-dancer-known-as-the-queen-of-swing-dies-at-99/2019/05/06/91876e40-700a-11e9-8be0-ca575670e91c_story.html), and the [Smithsonian Magazine](https://www.smithsonianmag.com/smart-news/norma-miller-queen-swing-has-died-99-180972125/)
