The First Stops routine was an early routine created at the Savoy Ballroom as an ensamble routine. 

You an read more about it's creation in [Norma Miller's](/historical_clips/norma_miller) book ["Swingin' at the Savoy"](https://books.google.com/books/about/Swingin_at_the_Savoy.html). 

It's called the First Stops now in comparison the the [Second Stops](/routines/second_stops) routine, it was originally just called the Stops routine.
