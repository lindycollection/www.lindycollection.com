The Sunday performace at Swingin' at the Savoy 2024 of the Stops Routine. Which was composed of the [First Stops](/routines/first_stops) and [Second Stops](/routines/second_stops) routines back to back.

This was taught at the event by Sugar Sullivan and Barbara Billups who were top competitors at the famous Harvest Moon Ball competitions in New York City in the 1950s.
