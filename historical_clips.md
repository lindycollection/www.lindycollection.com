This is a page about Historical Clips.

There are a number of historical clips which have survived which document the origins of Lindy Hop from back in the day. Many are embedded scenes in movies, as well as new reel clips. These are great resources to see the origins of the dance and many of the moves that we've learned over the years being done in the early days of the dance.
